# Return a conditional interval before promising replacement supply

Run the commands in README.md. Inspect data/bounds_requests.json and results/bounds/exercise.json.

A complete request names the historical year, import/mirror basis, authored alpha and beta, lost FAO origin, and outside-envelope gamma. The response gives lower/upper covered and unmet kg, the decision class and the unrestricted-outside upper bound. It explains that the accounting residual is not measured spare capacity.

Two saved requests return qualified intervals. A missing envelope or an unsupported year causes abstention. Guaranteed shipments, shortage probabilities, political priorities and arbitrary caller data are rejected. An interval whose upper endpoint covers demand but lower endpoint does not is unresolved without outside-capacity evidence, not a delivery commitment.

Explain why a zero modeled loss is not proof of zero physical trade, why a mirror amount is not independent truth, and why a source accounting gap cannot be relabeled as future spare supply. For a separate extension, accept supplier-specific dated availability/routing evidence with commodity compatibility and contract constraints. Preserve the frozen historical research and validate the new contract independently; the present interface does not certify arbitrary new data or a production service.
