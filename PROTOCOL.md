## Fixed authored capacity and outage grid

For target year Y and every one of the ten named origins k, remove all modeled deliveries from k. This is an authored complete-source-loss scenario, not a label of an actual export ban, war, weather event or observed shortage. Demands for replacement equal the qualified current-year quantities from k to each importer. Run both import-reported and exporter-mirror current quantities on the identical common-support edge set; prior-year routing limits and origin totals are the same in both bases.

For each remaining origin j, authored additional shared supply is alpha times its reported World exports in Y−1. Alpha is fixed at {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25}. This is a sensitivity assumption, not measured inventory, unused export capacity or a delivery promise. Additional routing capacity to importer i is beta times that pair's positive importer-reported Y−1 quantity, with beta in {0.5, 1, 2}; an unavailable prior link is not routed. The lost origin supplies no replacement. HS aggregation does not establish milling compatibility, freight capacity, contracts, harvest timing or political availability.

For each origin-loss/source-basis/alpha/beta configuration, compare the sum of three separate-country feasible replacement calculations (each can claim the same shared supplier pool) with a joint source–origin–importer–sink maximum flow. The joint objective maximizes covered kilograms, with replacement at each importer bounded by its source-specific modeled loss. It does not optimize fairness, nutrition, price, urgency or a national welfare function. Node/edge order is fixed by numeric country code; equal-total-flow allocations are not empirical priority recommendations. Retain every edge flow, importer demand/covered/unmet amount, supplier used/capacity, separate-country totals, joint total and overpromise gap. Report exact fractions and readable kg/tonne values with explicit conversions.

There are four alpha values, three beta values, ten source losses and two current source bases: 240 declared configurations per target year, including zero-loss and zero-supply sanity cases. No coefficient, threshold, objective, country priority, spare fraction or routing multiplier is learned, selected or tuned. Zero loss has undefined loss-normalized ratios, not manufactured perfect coverage. A feasible joint optimum cannot exceed summed independent-country optima by construction; the data-specific question is where and by how much they differ under these assumptions.
