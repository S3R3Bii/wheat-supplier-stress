## Source identity before quantities

Use only FAOSTAT's [Detailed trade matrix (TM)](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/TM/metadata) for bilateral flows and [Crops and livestock products (TCL)](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/TCL/metadata) for national totals. Never combine their values with UN Comtrade weights. Both official metadata pages describe annual physical trade quantities, generally net weight, in tonnes, and the same FAO country classifications. Imports include relevant food aid and re-imports; exports include re-exports. These compiled historical records are not contemporaneous operational delivery records.

The official [item definitions](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#definitions) bind item 15, Wheat, in both TM and TCL to HS07 100110/100190 and HS12 100111/100119/100191/100199: the grain heading 1001, not flour item 16 or wheat-product equivalents. The metadata describes the current classification through HS2017/CPC mapping; minor reclassification differences remain a stated source limitation, not an exact reproduction of the UN extracts.

The FAO area codes in data/planned_fao_network.json are source-specific. They are not assertions that FAO geographic codes and UN customs codes describe identical customs territories. Both metadata pages explicitly include French Guiana, Martinique, Guadeloupe and Reunion in France since 2007 and exclude French Polynesia and New Caledonia. Both also describe the reporting-coverage limitations for Russia and Ukraine. Preserve these qualifications. Country labels, codes and flags must agree within the FAO projections. Do not infer that a generic M49 label proves inclusion of Monaco, Puerto Rico or the US Virgin Islands; this version makes no such claim and makes no numerical comparison against the earlier customs-area extracts.

Observed domain update dates are TM December 23, 2025 and TCL July 24, 2026; both metadata revisions are December 22, 2025. Keep that difference visible. TCL supplies the publisher's total-trade denominator, not the sum of the ten selected partners. Shared definitions do not prove identical revision histories or accounting equality. The unchanged 80%–105% reconciliation gate applies, with all differences retained. No national total is constructed from an incomplete partner subset.

## Bounded acquisition

Use those ordinary controls only, with no hidden browser requests or copied application credentials. The four fixed downloads in data/planned_fao_network.json contain at most 179 distinct requested observations: 90 importer-reported bilateral quantities, 60 exporter mirror quantities, nine national importer totals and twenty prior national exporter totals. Select Wheat only, the specified quantity element only and the stated years/countries/partners only. Use table CSV with codes, units, flags, notes and null states where the interface supports them. Retain original files privately, hashes, UTC capture time, actual selected filters, download mechanism and this plan's commit. Default previews and unrelated search snippets are not cohort captures; do not press Show Data for an unbounded/default selection. No bulk ZIP, all-years extraction, paid query or general query service is used. The whole study, including the earlier UN requests and any public projection, remains below 10,000 original records.

Bind the returned schema before qualification, preserving actual field names, element/item/area/partner codes, numeric strings, unit and flag descriptions. Quantity must be explicitly tonnes; multiplying an exact decimal token by 1,000 only changes its computational unit to kg, not its measurement precision. Do not assume current values are rounded to whole tonnes from the older one-page guide. A flag not understood from official definitions is unavailable for modeling until resolved. Preserve positive publisher estimates and identify them as compiled/estimated; they are not independent corroboration of UN gaps. Zero, null, omitted and conflicting keys remain distinct unavailable states. Identical full-key duplicates can collapse, with a ledger; conflicting duplicates cannot.

## Unchanged analysis gates and boundary

Keep all three importers, ten origins, years, source-basis comparisons, alpha/beta values, source losses, optimization objective and independent min-cut checks from PROTOCOL.md. Require all nine positive unique national import totals and all twenty positive unique prior national export totals. An eligible current pair requires positive quantities on both the import and export sides. Each importer/current year must have at least two common origins and import-side common-support mass between 80% and 105% inclusive of its TCL national total. Each importer/prior year must have at least two positive prior routes. Missingness is not zero. Do not drop a country, year or origin after quantities, replace a national denominator, relax a threshold or treat a failed gate as a scenario finding.

If all gates pass, build separate FAO inputs and ledgers under data/fao/, run 2019 development only, and commit the protocol, native bindings, source checks, input matrices, algorithm/checker code, fixtures and development outputs before any 2020 scenarios. The original UN artifacts remain unchanged and clearly distinguished. A later FAO release would be a compiled-source scenario exercise, not validation that the UN attempt passed, not a new max-flow method, and not evidence of actual spare supply, food shortage probability, historical forecast accuracy or political priority.
